How It Pours

Five steps to after hours.

  1. 01 · Milk8 oz, any kind

    Dairy, almond, oat, banana — she isn’t picky.

  2. 02 · The Mix2 Tbsp Sheer & Udder

    One level scoop. Heaping is between you and your conscience.

  3. 03 · Spirit (optional)1½–2 oz

    Whiskey, rum, or whatever’s winking at you from the cabinet.

  4. 04 · FrothWhisk it into velvet

    Milk frother, whisk, blender — or a very determined spoon.

  5. 05 · SipAdult chocolate milk

    Hot, cold, or over ice. No crusts were cut in the making of this drink.
